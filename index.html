Questions

Can I personalise a box?

Yes. Add your names, colours or toppings in the notes at checkout. Personalised boxes cost £1 extra.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer collection and delivery. Choose one at checkout and we confirm the details with you.

How do I pay?

We accept cash or card. Collection orders may be paid in advance or on collection.

Do you have vegan options?

Vegan brownies and more cakes are coming soon. Message us for the latest.

Allergies?

Our bakes can contain milk, eggs, nuts and gluten. Tell us about any allergy before you order.
